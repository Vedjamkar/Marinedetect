Interactive geometry — drag the towfish and the target

Drag the towfish to change its altitude, the target to change its range, or the slider to change the object's height. The shadow length follows from the geometry alone. The pipeline runs this backwards — it measures the shadow in the image and solves for the height. The dashed grazing ray passes the top of the object and lands where the shadow ends; that is what ties shadow length to height. Assumes a locally flat seabed.
